Where every compiler in this repository came from.

Each tree below is an UNTOUCHED extraction of its upstream at the stated
commit. Nothing has been pruned, patched, or reformatted. Every later commit
in this repository is therefore, by construction, a visible change against a
known original -- which is the entire point of starting this way.

  gs1cc/   Golden Sun's compiler
           https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc
           04179d4a511b13cef92eacdb10a51bcd124fea7a
           gcc/version.c: "2.96 20000731 (experimental)"
           The 2000-07-31 development snapshot. Red Hat built gcc-2.96-54 on
           this same base (their gcc-2.96-20000731.tar.bz2 is byte-identical
           to it for arm.c, arm.md, combine.c, loop.c and cse.c), so two
           independent parties selected it.

  gs2cc/   The Lost Age's compiler
           https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc
           b58080295efffd79bc0875cd425bb1d232c41ccf  (tag releases/gcc-3.0.0)
           gcc/version.c: "3.0"
           Confirmed by measurement rather than assumption: of the 3.0.x
           releases, 3.0.0 matches the previously-carried tree best by a wide
           margin (1441 identical files of 1464 shared, against 1332 for
           3.0.1 and fewer for later releases).

  agbcc/   the stock Thumb audio compiler (old_agbcc)
           https://github.com/pret/agbcc
           da598c1d918402c42c0c0d7128ba14567f3175e9
           gcc/version.c: "2.9-arm-000512"
           Also confirmed by measurement: every commit in pret/agbcc's history
           was compared against the previously-carried tree, and da598c1d is
           the best match.

WHY THIS REPOSITORY WAS RESTARTED

The previous history descended from a third-party fork. Its compilers had been
patched before they ever arrived here, and nothing recorded what had changed,
so "stock" could not be distinguished from "someone's edit". Auditing it after
the fact turned up, among other things: 120 invented compiler options in the
2.96 tree, an invented -mcamelot-gs2 in the 3.0 tree, and TEN invented options
in agbcc -- three of which were declared and never referenced by anything.

Starting from untouched upstream makes that class of problem impossible to
reproduce: anything that is not upstream is in a commit, with a message.
